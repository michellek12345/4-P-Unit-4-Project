The Unofficial Local Guide to Chelsea and Hudson Yards !

The best shopping areas !

Here are some of the best areas in chelsea where you can shop for what you want !

  1. Chelsea Market! You can support all kinds of local and small businesses but you can still go to larger stores if they contain what you are looking for
  2. Hudson Yards Mall!, here you can find stores like muji, h&m, and more to look for things you may need, the stores are very spacious as well so you can definietly find what you are looking for, you can also enjoy the scenery and decorations they put out every year for different holidays
  3. Stands around both areas! You can usually find a stand or a group of stands around the area or within both locations where you can also find what you are looking for.
